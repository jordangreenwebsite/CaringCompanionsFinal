This Cookie Policy explains how Caring Companions Pet Sitting & Dog Walking uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or pieces of information that websites can store on your device when you visit them.

They can be used for purposes such as making a website work correctly, remembering preferences, understanding how a website is used and, on some websites, providing advertising.

How we use cookies

Caring Companions aims to keep the use of cookies and similar technologies to a minimum.

Our website may use technologies that are necessary for the website to function correctly, securely and reliably.

We may also use limited website analytics to understand information such as which pages are visited and how visitors find and use our website.

Where consent or an opportunity to object is legally required for a particular technology, we will provide the appropriate controls.

Essential technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for features of the website to operate correctly or securely.

These may include technologies used for website security, form functionality or remembering choices you have made.

Where a technology is strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested, your consent is not required.

Website analytics

We may use website analytics to understand how visitors use our website and help us improve it.

Where analytics or similar technologies require your consent, they will not be activated until you have made your choice.

Where an applicable legal exception allows analytics without prior consent, we will provide the information and controls required by law.

Third-party services

Our website may link to or use third-party services such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and Formspree.

When you choose to interact with a third-party service, that provider may process information according to its own privacy and cookie policies.

Simply clicking a normal link to another website does not mean that Caring Companions places that website’s cookies on your device.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your web browser settings.

Where our website provides cookie or privacy controls, you can also use those controls to manage your preferences.

Blocking some essential technologies may affect how parts of the website function.

Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if the technologies or services used by our website change.

The latest version will always be available on this page.
