Caring Companions Pet Sitting & Dog Walking respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it and how it is used when you visit our website, contact us or use our pet-care services.

1. Who We Are

Caring Companions Pet Sitting & Dog Walking provides dog walking, pet drop-in visits, overnight pet sitting and wedding dog chaperone services.

Caring Companions is operated by Tash and is based in Rhymney, South Wales.

Contact details:

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact us using the details above.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you contact us or use our services, we may collect information including:

  • Your name
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Home address or postcode
  • Information about the services you require
  • Booking dates and times
  • Information about your pets, including their names, breed, age, behaviour, routines and care requirements
  • Information you choose to provide through our website enquiry form, WhatsApp, email, telephone or social media
  • Information required to safely provide pet-care services at your home

We may also collect limited technical information when you use our website, such as your browser type, device information and website usage information where relevant analytics or security tools are used.

3. Why We Use Your Information

We may use your personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Provide quotations or guide prices
  • Arrange meet and greets
  • Manage pet-care bookings
  • Provide dog walking, pet sitting and other services
  • Contact you regarding your booking
  • Understand your pet’s individual care requirements
  • Maintain appropriate business and accounting records
  • Improve our services and website
  • Deal with questions, complaints or disputes
  • Meet legal, insurance or regulatory requirements

4. Our Lawful Basis for Using Your Information

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for collecting and using personal information.

Depending on the circumstances, we may rely on:

Contract — where information is necessary to provide a service you have requested or to take steps before entering into a service agreement.

Legitimate interests — where it is reasonably necessary to operate and protect Caring Companions, respond to customers, maintain records and improve our services.

Legal obligation — where we are required to retain or provide information to comply with applicable law.

Consent — where you have specifically agreed to a particular use of your information and consent is the appropriate lawful basis.

5. Information About Your Pets

We collect information about pets so we can understand their needs and provide appropriate care.

This may include information about feeding, behaviour, routines, medication, veterinary requirements or other relevant care instructions.

Some of this information may indirectly relate to you as the pet owner and will therefore be handled securely alongside your other personal information.

6. Website Enquiries

When you complete an enquiry form on our website, the information you provide may include your name, telephone number, email address, postcode, pet details, required service and preferred dates.

This information is used to respond to your enquiry and assess whether Caring Companions can provide the requested service.

Website enquiries may be delivered through third-party form-processing or communication services such as Formspree or WhatsApp.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7. WhatsApp and Social Media

If you contact Caring Companions using WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or another third-party platform, information will also be processed by the company operating that service.

We recommend reviewing the relevant platform’s privacy information if you would like to understand how it handles your data.

8. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information.

Where necessary, information may be shared with trusted third parties that help us operate our business, including:

  • Website and form-processing providers
  • Email and communication providers
  • Payment or accounting providers
  • IT and website service providers
  • Insurers or professional advisers
  • Veterinary professionals or emergency services where necessary to protect an animal
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by law

We only share information where there is a legitimate reason to do so.

9. International Processing

Some third-party technology providers used by our website or communications services may process information outside the United Kingdom.

Where this applies, we take reasonable steps to use reputable providers and appropriate data-protection safeguards.

10. How Long We Keep Your Information

We only keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary.

Enquiry information that does not result in a booking may be deleted when it is no longer required.

Customer and booking records may be retained for longer where required for accounting, insurance, legal or business-record purposes.

We regularly consider whether information still needs to be retained.

11. How We Protect Your Information

Reasonable steps are taken to protect personal information against accidental loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

Access to customer information is limited to people who reasonably require it to provide or administer Caring Companions services.

12. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights including the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of personal information held about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete certain information
  • Ask us to restrict how certain information is used
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Ask for certain information to be transferred
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Some rights only apply in particular circumstances.

13. Marketing

We will not add you to marketing communications simply because you make a pet-care enquiry.

Where consent is required for marketing, we will ask for it separately.

You may ask us to stop sending marketing communications at any time.

14. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for the website to operate.

If we use analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies, appropriate information and consent options will be provided where required.

15. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services such as Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp.

Caring Companions is not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party websites.

16. Complaints

If you have concerns about how Caring Companions uses your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve the issue.

You also have the right to raise a concern with the UK’s data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

More information is available from the ICO at ico.org.uk.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be published on this website.
